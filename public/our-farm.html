The Farm

Rooted in
Marwar.

“Purity is a process.”

Rudraksha Farm began with a simple intention: make ghee we would be proud to serve at home.

Our farm is in Barmer, Rajasthan. The focus is not simply on selling more; it is on maintaining a traditional chain of work from cow and milk to curd, makkhan and finally ghee.

The farm story is deliberately simple: take care at every stage, keep the process visible, and let the product speak for itself.

Farm to Jar

A traditional
journey.

01

Gir Cow Milk

Milk is collected from our desi cows by hand as part of the farm's traditional process.

02

Heat & Cool

The milk is heated using the farm's gobar-gas system, then cooled before setting.

03

Earthen Pot Curd

The cooled milk is set into curd in an earthen vessel.

04

Bilona Churning

The next day, curd is hand churned to make makkhan.

05

Ghee · Filter · Jar

The makkhan is clarified into ghee, filtered and packed for the kitchen.

RUDRAKSHA FARM · THE SOUL OF TRADITION

Good ghee begins long before it reaches the jar.

Our farm is in Barmer, Rajasthan, where we follow a traditional journey from cow’s milk to curd, makkhan and finally ghee.

Our intention is simple: care at every stage, respect for the process, and a product we are proud to serve at home.

From our farm to your home — with tradition at heart.
Farm to Jar

A traditional
journey.

01

Gir Cow Milk

Milk is collected from our desi cows by hand as part of the farm's traditional process.

02

Heat & Cool

The milk is heated using the farm's gobar-gas system, then cooled before setting.

03

Earthen Pot Curd

The cooled milk is set into curd in an earthen vessel.

04

Bilona Churning

The next day, curd is hand churned to make makkhan.

05

Ghee · Filter · Jar

The makkhan is clarified into ghee, filtered and packed for the kitchen.

01 / The Brand

Purity is a
process.

We didn't build Rudraksha Farm to make another jar of ghee. We built it to bring a traditional process back into the everyday kitchen.

From our farm in Barmer, Rajasthan, milk is collected by hand, traditionally heated, cooled and set into curd. The next morning, the curd is hand churned by the Bilona method. The resulting makkhan is then transformed into ghee, clarified, filtered and packed.

No shortcuts in the story. No need to make the story louder than the product.

— The Rudraksha Farm team

03 / The Bilona Method

Five steps.
One old idea.

A traditional chain where every stage matters.

01

Hand Milking

Milk is collected from the cows by hand rather than through an automated milking line.

02

Traditional Heating

The milk is heated using the farm's gobar-gas based system.

03

Curd in Earthenware

After cooling, the milk is set into curd in a traditional earthen vessel.

04

Bilona Churning

The curd is hand churned the next morning to separate makkhan.

05

Clarify · Filter · Pack

The makkhan is processed into ghee, then filtered and packed into jars.

Barmer · Rajasthan
04 / From the Farm

Born in
Marwar.

“Achha bechne par focus hai.”

We started with a simple intention: give people access to ghee we would be proud to put in our own kitchens. Growth matters—but the product comes first.

Rudraksha Farm is our attempt to build a modern dairy brand without losing the visual warmth and patience of traditional Indian food culture.

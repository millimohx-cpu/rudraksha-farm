Refunds & Cancellations

Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation as soon as possible after placing an order. Cancellation requests should be made before the order is dispatched. Once dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.

Damaged or Incorrect Delivery

If a parcel arrives damaged or contains an incorrect product, contact Rudraksha Farm promptly with the order reference and supporting photographs.

Food Product Returns

For hygiene and food-safety reasons, opened or used food products are not eligible for ordinary returns. Unopened products may be reviewed case-by-case when there is a valid issue.

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will be initiated to the original payment method. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank.

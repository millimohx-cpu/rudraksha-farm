For cancellation requests, contact us as soon as possible on WhatsApp. Cancellation may not be possible after an order has been dispatched.

If a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated to the original payment method. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank.

If a parcel arrives damaged, leaked or incorrect, contact us promptly with the order ID and clear photos/videos of the package and product. We will review the issue and provide the applicable resolution.

Product quality concerns

For genuine product-quality concerns, contact us with the order details so the matter can be reviewed.
